Who We Are

PayRoll Protect provides construction payroll verification services. Contact: hello@payrollprotect.co.uk, Romford, London.

Website Visitors

The booking form is provided by GoHighLevel (LeadConnector), the system we use to manage enquiries. If you send it, your details are stored there and we use them to respond to your enquiry. We do not sell personal data.

Biometric Data on Client Sites

  • Biometric templates are encrypted and stored on UK-based servers.
  • Workers give consent at onboarding. A Data Processing Agreement is available to clients.
  • Biometric data is not shared with third parties, and is deleted on request or at contract end.

Cookies

This website sets no cookies of its own. Its fonts and scripts are served from this website, so viewing it sends no data to third parties, except on the booking page: its form loads from GoHighLevel (LeadConnector), which may set its own cookies.

Your Rights

You can ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing hello@payrollprotect.co.uk.
