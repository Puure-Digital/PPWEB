How These Figures Are Worked Out.

Plain arithmetic on your answers, worked out in your browser.

  1. Every year

    £180,000 a week × 5% × 52 weeks = £468,000

  2. Unverified hours

    £9,000 a week ÷ £32 an hour = 281 hours a week

  3. Per site

    £468,000 ÷ 1 site = £468,000 a year

  4. The pilot

    £180,000 a week ÷ 1 site × 5% × 76 days ÷ 7 = £97,714

    Live on site from day 14 to day 90: 76 days of checks.
