An estimated £1.4 billion every year in the UK construction market falls to fraudulent financial activity. PayRoll Protect is here to stop it, by verifying 100% of your workforce, daily.

Because every subcontractor invoice is a claim. Most are checked against paper registers and spreadsheets, if they’re checked at all. And at summary level, the numbers always look right.

Ghost workers who never scan at the terminal. Hours that outrun the shift. One person clocking in for another. Each one hides inside a total that looks right.

PayRoll Protect puts a face-scanning terminal on site. Every worker clocks in and out with their face. No cards, no apps, no proxy sign-ins.

Setup is simple. The terminal arrives pre-configured and mounts at site entry. We install a Starlink dish so the terminal never relies on site Wi-Fi or mobile signal. Workers enrol on their first day with nothing to download, and most sites are live within two weeks. And if the link ever drops, scans queue on the terminal and sync the moment it’s back.

Then every line of every labour invoice is matched against the terminal record. Anything without a scan behind it is held for query before payroll runs.

✦How it works

From Terminal Scan to Approved Payment.

  1. 01

    Worker Verified

    Identity confirmed at a fixed device on site entry: face or fingerprint, with GPS location. No cards, PINs or proxy sign-ins.

  2. 02

    Hours Captured

    In and out times recorded per worker, per day. Works offline and syncs when the connection returns.

  3. 03

    Invoice Checked

    Every claimed line is compared against the verified attendance record.

  4. 04

    Exceptions Raised

    Anything without a matching record is flagged before payroll, grouped by type.

  5. 05

    Payment Released

    Only verified labour is paid. Every payment carries its evidence.
